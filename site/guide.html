idiom · field guide

v0.7.0 · design acceptance for AI-built software

What it is, in one breath

Your coding agent builds fast. Nobody looks at every screen in every condition. Idiom opens your real, running app, walks every state you declare — the expired link, the error message, mobile, dark mode — judges what rendered against your own design rules, and turns problems into small, provable fixes. Then it remembers what you decided, so the same argument never happens twice.

The pitch — why try it

It sees what you don't

Crawlers and your own eyes see the happy path. Idiom drives fixture-fed states no crawler can reach — expired, taken, invalid, empty — at every viewport, in light and dark, on web pages and desktop (Electron) apps.

Evidence, not opinion

Screenshots tied to a commit. Findings that cite the rule they break. Before/after proof a fix landed. Reports that say what was not checked. Never a 0–100 score.

It converges

Reject a finding once — "that's intentional" — and it never comes back. Accepted taste becomes rules fed to your agent before it writes code. Review tools nag; Idiom learns.

How it works — the loop

  1. Learn

    idiom init mines your repo, interviews you for what code can't answer, and writes PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md — your product's design law.

  2. Render

    idiom capture starts your app for real and photographs every declared state × viewport × color scheme, keyed to the exact commit.

  3. Critique

    idiom review runs ten measurable checks (overflow, contrast, tap targets, console errors, breaks in your product's own spacing rhythm…) plus a model judge armed with your law. Five findings max.

  4. Repair

    You accept or reject each finding. Accepted ones become bounded tickets — or paste-ready instructions for Claude Code, Lovable, Cursor, whatever builds your code.

  5. Verify

    idiom verify re-renders the same states after the fix and proves the finding is gone. No model — the exit code is the verdict. Wire it into CI.

  6. Remember

    Every decision is logged forever. idiom context hands the accumulated judgment to the next agent before it writes a line of UI.

Commands

CommandWhat it does
The doorway — zero setup
idiom quick <url…>Review any running page (localhost works): desktop + mobile, light + dark, all measurable checks, an HTML report — and an honest list of what it couldn't reach without setup. Two minutes, no config.
Setting up
idiom initScaffold PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md, and .idiom/. Interactive form interviews you; can also wire itself into an autobuild pipeline. --yes for scripts.
idiom validate [flow]Check the config and flow files are well-formed before anything runs.
idiom reference addPin images or URLs of designs you're aiming for — or, with --anti, ones to avoid. The review holds your app against them.
The loop
idiom capture <flow>Drive the real app — a web page or an Electron desktop window — through every declared state and store screenshots, DOM, console, and accessibility data, tied to the commit.
idiom review <flow>Judge a capture: deterministic checks + model critique against your design law, references, and past decisions.
idiom report [run]Open the self-contained HTML report — printable, sendable, states its own coverage and limits.
idiom decideAccept or reject findings, with reasons. Recorded permanently; rule-scope rejections suppress the pattern forever.
idiom rulingAnswer the design questions a review raised ("you style inputs three ways — which is law?"). Your answer is appended to DESIGN.md.
idiom instructionRender one accepted finding as plain paste-ready text for any coding tool or chat.
idiom ticketsEmit accepted findings as bounded repair tickets into your tracker's triage.
idiom verify <flow>After the fix merges: re-render, compare before/after, exit 0 only if the finding is provably gone. Model-free, CI-safe.
Improving — when the law doesn't exist yet
idiom explore <flow>Give one state and one sentence of dissatisfaction; get three real rendered directions of your own app to choose between. Choosing writes a rule into your design law — the rule, not a patch, is the product. Repair tickets offered, declinable.
idiom explore resolveRecord your choice (or an explicit no-choice) against a completed exploration run later — the run survives even if the process, terminal, or session didn't.
Memory, made visible
idiom systemOpen your design system as a visual page: token palette as swatches, the colors and type that actually shipped, your rules, your rulings, your standing rejections with evidence, and what's covered.
idiom context [flow]Print the accumulated design brief — law, rulings, prohibitions, tokens, open work — sized to paste into any agent before it writes UI.

What it deliberately is not

  • Not a design generator. Figma, Claude Design, v0 decide what should exist. Idiom proves what shipped meets the standard — and when it renders alternatives (explore), they are variants of your own running app, and the output is a rule in your law, never a mockup.
  • Not a grader. No 0–100 score to game. It reports what it judged, what it found, and what it could not see.
  • Not a cloud service. A local CLI with documented file formats. Your evidence stays in your repo; any agent or pipeline can drive it.
