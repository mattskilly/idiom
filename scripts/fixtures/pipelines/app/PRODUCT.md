# Product context

## Target user

A guest who wants to book a table at a small neighbourhood restaurant.

## Job to be done

Find a free time and reserve it in under a minute.

## Flow objective

The home page explains booking and leads to the time picker.

## What must be obvious

How to start a booking, and how to book a large party.

## Success criteria

Every line is readable in light and dark, and the button is the first action.

## Hard constraints

Text meets WCAG AA contrast.
