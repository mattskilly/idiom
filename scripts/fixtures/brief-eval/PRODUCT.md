# Page editor

A visual editor for building landing pages. People drag blocks from the Add
drawer onto the canvas, then edit text and images in place.

## Users

Marketers who build several pages a week, mostly on a laptop, sometimes on a
tablet. They reuse the same handful of blocks on almost every page.

## Principles

- The canvas gets the space; panels stay compact.
- Every action works with a mouse, a keyboard and touch.
